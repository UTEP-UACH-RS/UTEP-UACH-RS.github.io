Remote Sensing Research

The RS & STEM Education project investigates methods for integrating satellite imagery, unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV), and ground-based hyperspectral observations for environmental and agricultural applications. In particular, this research emphasizes image acquisition, calibration, multi-resolution data fusion and enhancement, quantitative validation, image classification, while providing students with hands-on experience in image processing and remote sensing.

Research Areas

  • Satellite remote sensing
  • UAV RGB and hyperspectral imaging
  • Vegetation and crop-health monitoring
  • Soil and irrigation analysis
  • Image registration and data fusion
  • Super-resolution and spatial enhancement
  • Spectral analysis and calibration
