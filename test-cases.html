Remote Sensing Test Cases

The following test cases provide opportunities for students to apply remote-sensing concepts and image-processing techniques to agricultural and environmental problems.

Test Case 1: Agricultural Field Analysis

Analyze Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery to investigate vegetation conditions using vegetation indices within selected agricultural fields.

Test Case 2: Multi-Resolution Image Comparison

Compare Sentinel-2 satellite imagery with higher-resolution NAIP aerial imagery and investigate how spatial resolution affects the representation of agricultural features.
