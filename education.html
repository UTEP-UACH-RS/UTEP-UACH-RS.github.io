STEM Education

The education component emphasizes practical learning through field activities, remote-sensing data analysis, programming, GIS, and image processing.

Student Activities

  • Satellite-data acquisition and interpretation
  • UAV image collection and mapping
  • Hyperspectral and multispectral analysis
  • Vegetation-index calculations
  • Python and MATLAB processing exercises
  • Research reports, posters, and presentations
