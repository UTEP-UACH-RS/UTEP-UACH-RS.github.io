Course Title: Introduction to Remote Sensing

Level: Senior Undergraduate or graduate level

Prerequisites: University Physics II and Calculus I. Signals and Systems is recommended.

Course Description:

Introduction to the principles and applications of remote sensing with emphasis on electromagnetic radiation, optical sensing systems, satellite and airborne imaging, multispectral and hyperspectral sensing, and digital image processing. Topics include spatial, spectral, radiometric, and temporal resolution; image acquisition; radiometric and geometric corrections; vegetation indices; image classification; (and multisensor data fusion). Laboratory activities introduce students to the processing and interpretation of satellite, aerial imagery, and groud-level using datasets and remote sensing software.

Student Activities

  • Satellite-data sourcing, NDVI index calculation and spatial vegetation mapping (at regional level)
  • UAV image collection, image processing, ExG index calculation (at local level)
  • Hyperspectral acquisition, image calibration, NDVI calculation and image analysis (at micro level)
  • Python (Google Colab) and ENVI® 5.6 (NV5 Geospatial Solutions) image processing tools
