STEM Education

The education component emphasizes practical learning through image acquisition, remote-sensing, data analysis, and image interpretation.

Student Activities

  • Satellite-data sourcing, NDVI index calculation and spatial vegetation mapping (at regional level)
  • UAV image collection, image processing, ExG index calculation (at local level)
  • Hyperspectral acquisition, image calibration, NDVI calculation and image analysis (at micro level)
  • Python (Google Colab) and ENVI® 5.6 (NV5 Geospatial Solutions) image processing tools

Remote Sensing Laboratories

A series of hands-on laboratory activities has been developed to introduce students to fundamental concepts in remote sensing and digital image processing. The laboratories use satellite and aerial imagery to explore multispectral imaging, vegetation indices, and multi-sensor image analysis.

The laboratory activities progressively introduce students to the processing and analysis of Sentinel-2 satellite images, aerial and UAV imagery.
