STEM Education

The education component emphasizes practical learning through image acquisition, remote-sensing, data analysis, and image interpretation.

Student Activities

  • Satellite-data sourcing, NDVI index calculation and spatial vegetaion mapping (at regional level)
  • UAV image collection, image processing, ExG index calculation (at local level)
  • Hyperspectral acquisition, image calibration, NDVI calculation and image analysis (at micro level)
  • Python (Google Colab), ENVI, and MATLAB image processing tools

Suggested Textbook

The experiments and educational activities presented on this website can be used together with selected readings from the following textbook.

Figure 1. Remote Sensing Digital Image Analysis, Sixth Edition.

Remote Sensing Digital Image Analysis

Author: John A. Richards
Edition: Sixth Edition
Publisher: Springer
Year: 2022

This textbook provides background information for many of the remote sensing concepts used in the work described on this website. Selected chapters are recommended so students can review the theory before working with the data.

Recommended Chapters

  • Chapter 1: Introduction to remote sensing image data, sensors, spectral bands, and image characteristics.
  • Chapter 2: Image correction and registration.
  • Chapter 4: Improving and preparing images for analysis.
  • Chapters 5 and 7: Spatial processing of images.
  • Chapter 6: Working with spectral bands and image transformations, including concepts useful for vegetation indices.
  • Chapter 12: Combining and comparing information collected from different sensors.
