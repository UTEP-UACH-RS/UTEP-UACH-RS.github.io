Collaboration Opportunities

The project welcomes opportunities for interdisciplinary and international collaboration in areas including:

  • Satellite, UAV, and ground-based remote sensing
  • Precision agriculture and environmental monitoring
  • Hyperspectral, multispectral, RGB, and thermal imaging
  • Image calibration, registration, and data fusion
  • Spatial-resolution enhancement
  • Development of remote-sensing educational activities
  • Undergraduate and graduate student research

Interested in Working With Us?

Students interested in participating in research activities and faculty or organizations interested in collaboration are encouraged to contact the project team.

When contacting us, please include a brief description of your interests and the type of collaboration or research activity you would like to explore.
