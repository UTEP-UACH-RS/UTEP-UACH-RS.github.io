About the Project

This project develops educational and research activities that combine remote sensing, precision agriculture, and STEM education. The goal is to provide students with practical experience using satellite data, UAV imagery, field measurements, and image-processing tools for agricultural monitoring.

Objectives

  • Develop hands-on remote-sensing educational activities.
  • Integrate satellite, UAV, and field observations.
  • Apply image processing and data fusion to agricultural problems.
  • Strengthen collaborative STEM education and research.
